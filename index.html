About Sprite City

The game, the Sprite City token, and how points convert.

About

Sprite City is a top-down city game on Robinhood Chain. Hunt stock-type sprites, duel rival hunters in the arena, and run timed deliveries across nine districts. Playing earns points, and points become the Sprite City token when it launches. The city runs on a day/night cycle — dusk is golden hour, when golden sprites appear far more often, Psychic and Steel sprites favor the night, and deliveries completed at night pay +25%.

The Token
  • Chain: Robinhood Chain
  • Total supply: 1,000,000,000 — fixed, minted once at launch, no further minting
  • Fair launch, no presale
  • Premium shop tier priced in the token after launch
  • The contract address will be published ONLY on spritecity.world and @SpriteCityHQ on X. Anything published elsewhere is not official.
Points & Conversion
  • Season rate: 100 points = 1 Sprite City token, announced before each season and never changed mid-season
  • Rewards are paid from a pre-funded reward pool wallet (address published at launch) — points never mint tokens
  • Withdrawals open at the token launch; a wallet is needed only to withdraw
Playtest Claim

Playtest points still count. Once sign-in opens, claim once per account — up to 5,000 points will be recognized, at a bonus rate of 5 points = 1 Sprite City token (up to 1,000 Sprite City tokens per account). Playtest claimants also receive the OG Hunter badge and a 1.5× points multiplier for all of Season 1. The claim window is 7 days.

Fair Play

Withdrawals pass a gate of checks before they are paid: one wallet per account, a minimum points threshold, account age & activity checks, and rate limits. Flagged points are held for review.
