1. About Sprite City

Sprite City is a top-down city game on Robinhood Chain. You explore a living mini-city — on foot, by car, or by motorbike — hunting wild sprites: creatures whose types mirror well-known stocks. You catch them, collect them, duel rival hunters in a side-view arena, and run timed delivery missions across the districts.

Playing earns points. Points are the game's score today and become convertible into the Sprite City token when the token launches. This paper explains exactly how that works — the rates, the caps, and the rules — so nothing about the economy is a surprise later.

2. The game

  • Hunt & catch. Five sprite species roam the city. Weaken a sprite in its catch battle (or stun it with the Orb Blaster), then throw catch orbs to collect it.
  • Arena duels. Rival hunters roam the map. Bump into one to duel: a best-of-3 side-view fight with punches, kicks, blocks, and orb shots — land the finisher for a bonus.
  • City Run deliveries. Pick up a package and beat the clock to the destination. Pay is route points plus a time bonus, multiplied by your on-time streak (up to ×3).
  • Drive & ride. Parked cars and motorbikes wait at the roadside, and traffic cars brake when you step in front of them. Driving is fast — and raises your wanted stars.
  • Daily rhythm. A daily check-in streak (50 → 500 pts over 7 days) and four daily missions worth up to +470 pts in total.
  • Districts & the golden sprite. Nine districts to discover, and a rare golden sprite that appears now and then — worth +500 pts if you catch it before it flees.

3. Points

Points are earned by playing: catches, duel wins, deliveries, missions, check-ins, and exploration. During the playtest, points are stored on your device so anyone can jump in instantly — no account, no wallet.

When player accounts open (before the token launch), points become server-side: earned in the game, recorded by the server, and withdrawable as tokens. See section 6 for how playtest points are honored.

4. The Sprite City token

Chain: Robinhood Chain

Total supply: 1,000,000,000 — fixed, minted once at launch. No further minting, ever.

Launch: fair launch. No presale.

Utility: the premium tier of the in-game shop is priced in the token after launch, and points convert into the token for withdrawal (section 5).

The token has not launched yet. Its contract address will be published only on spritecity.world and on the game's official X account, @SpriteCityHQ. Any contract address published anywhere else is not official.

5. Conversion & the reward pool

  • Season rate: 100 points = 1 Sprite City token. The rate for a season is announced before that season starts and never changes mid-season.
  • Rewards are paid from a pre-funded reward pool. Points do not mint tokens. Before each season, the pool wallet is funded with tokens, and conversions are paid out of it. The pool wallet address is published at launch so anyone can verify its balance.
  • Withdrawals open at the token launch. You connect a wallet only when you withdraw — playing never requires one.
  • A healthy, active player earns roughly 400–600 points a day from regular play.

6. The playtest claim

Playtest points count. When player accounts open:

  • Sign in, then claim your playtest progress once per account.
  • Up to 5,000 playtest points per account are recognized.
  • Playtest claims convert at a bonus rate of 50 points = 1 token — up to 100 tokens per account, double the regular season rate.
  • Playtest claimants receive the OG Hunter badge and a 1.5× points multiplier for all of Season 1.
  • Player accounts open on Monday, November 23, 2026. The claim window is 7 days from that day — November 23 to November 30, 2026. Unclaimed playtest points expire after that; points earned after claiming are server-side only.

7. Fair play

Rewards attract bots, so the checks sit at the withdrawal gate, where they belong:

  • One wallet per account; a minimum points threshold to withdraw.
  • Account age and activity checks, plus rate limits on claims.
  • Flagged points are held for review instead of paid.

Playing itself stays open and frictionless — no wallet, no sign-up wall during the playtest.

9. FAQ

Do I need a wallet to play?

No. You only connect a wallet when you withdraw tokens.

Are my playtest points wasted?

No — see section 6. Claim them once accounts open: up to 5,000 points, at the 50:1 bonus rate, within the 7-day window.

When does the token launch?

On Monday, November 30, 2026, on Robinhood Chain. Player accounts and the playtest claim open one week earlier, on Monday, November 23, 2026. The contract address will be published only on spritecity.world and the official X account — anywhere else first is not official.

Can points be bought?

No. Points are earned by playing only.
